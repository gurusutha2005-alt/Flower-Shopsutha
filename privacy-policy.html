Privacy Policy

Your privacy is important to us. This page explains how Blossom Boutique collects, uses, stores and protects your personal information.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect information including your name, email address, phone number, delivery address and order details when you place an order or contact us.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to process flower orders, arrange deliveries, respond to inquiries, improve our services and send promotional offers if you choose to subscribe.

3. Data Protection

We implement reasonable security measures to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, disclosure or misuse.

4. Cookies

Our website may use cookies to improve your browsing experience, remember your preferences, analyze website traffic and provide better services. You can disable cookies in your browser settings.

5. Third-Party Services

We may use trusted third-party services such as Google Maps, Google Analytics and secure payment providers to improve our website functionality. These providers may process limited information according to their own privacy policies.

6. Marketing Communications

If you subscribe to our newsletter, we may send promotional emails about new flower collections, special offers and seasonal discounts. You can unsubscribe at any time.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to request access, correction or deletion of your personal information. You may also request that we stop processing your personal data where legally applicable.
