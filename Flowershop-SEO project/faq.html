Flower Care Questions

How can I keep flowers fresh longer?

Keep flowers in clean water, trim stems regularly and avoid direct sunlight.

How often should I change flower water?

Changing water regularly helps maintain freshness and prevents bacteria growth.

Can flowers be preserved?

Some flowers can be dried or preserved using special techniques.

Which flowers last the longest?

Flowers like roses, lilies and orchids can stay fresh longer with proper care.
